## Found during the sweep

- 2026-10-18 — **a panel crashed when search opened a Bookmarks board** (React #185, *Panel A crashed*):
  searching "Philosopher's Stone" from Routines opened Bookmarks and jumped to the bookmark. Two loops, both
  fixed: `dragSystem.useDragDrop` called setState after every render of every row even when the handle was
  unchanged; `virtualRows`' grid measure resized lines on sub-pixel wobble and re-ran without end. Crashed
  2 of 3 runs before, 0 of 8 after (5 local, 3 prod).
- Probe note: the settings forms' tabs switch on a pointer PRESS — `element.click()` from a script does nothing
  (every tab screenshot showed the first tab). Use a real mouse click (Playwright `locator.click()`).
