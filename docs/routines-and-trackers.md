## Trackers

Every tile and what it measures. Indented containers render inside their parent. Container names shown are the stable ones — on screen they read "Today's …" and follow the page's date filter.

### Stats

| Tile | What it tracks | Fed by |
|---|---|---|
| **Completed Tasks** | How many non-routine items you completed today, across the whole Schedule. Routines are counted separately as habits. | Completed Tasks, Task Countdown |
| **Completed Habits** | How many of your routine actions you completed today (anything from the Routines catalog). | Completed Habits |
| **Now** | The current time, and how much of the day is left. | _nothing_ |
| **Streak** | Consecutive days with at least one completion, plus your best run. | Current Streak |
