# Notes

## 1️⃣ Male ↔ Female (Masculine & Feminine Energies)

**Internal:**

- Logic / drive / action ↔ intuition / receptivity / reflection
- Integration: balanced decision-making, creativity, emotional regulation
- Psychological correlate: anima/animus, Jungian integration

**External:**

- How you relate to others (empathy, assertiveness)
- Relationships (respecting others’ boundaries while asserting yours)
- Expression of balanced energy in social, creative, and sexual contexts

## 2️⃣ Shadow ↔ Light (Darkness → Consciousness)

**Internal:**

- Repressed traits, shame, trauma ↔ conscious awareness, self-acceptance
- Integration: post-traumatic growth, turning impulses inward for refinement
- Psychological correlate: shadow work, CBT reframing

**External:**

- Actions aligned with awareness instead of projection
- Turning the other cheek, compassionate responses instead of reactive aggression
- Manifesting “internal light” in your behavior and relationships

## 3️⃣ Ego ↔ Self (Constructed Identity ↔ True Self)

**Internal:**

- Ego: conditioned identity, fears, false narratives
- Self: integrated, authentic, coherent consciousness
- Integration: recognizing false self-stories and acting from values, not impulses

**External:**

- Behavior consistent with internal values rather than social masks
- Integrity in work, relationships, habits

## 4️⃣ Instinct ↔ Intellect (Impulse ↔ Reason)

**Internal:**

- Raw desire, emotion, drives ↔ rational thought, planning, reflection
- Integration: conscious choice of action informed by both instinct and intellect

**External:**

- Strategic, skillful action in the world without repression or overreaction
- Aligning your creativity, productivity, and discipline with your instincts

## 5️⃣ Active ↔ Receptive / Effort ↔ Ease (Daoist Yin-Yang, Wu Wei)

**Internal:**

- Striving, forcing change ↔ allowing, flowing
- Integration: knowing when to act vs when to yield

**External:**

- Interactions and productivity that are balanced — effortful when needed, effortless when possible
- Harmonizing “hustle” with rest and flow

## 6️⃣ Body ↔ Mind / Spirit ↔ Matter (Embodiment)

**Internal:**

- Physical sensations, energy, health ↔ thoughts, emotions, meaning
- Integration: embodied practice, meditation, movement, somatic awareness

**External:**

- Physical discipline (exercise, nutrition, posture) aligned with mental clarity
- Creative and expressive output as a bridge between inner and outer world

## 7️⃣ Self ↔ Other / Inner ↔ Outer

**Internal:**

- Aligning your internal state with values, needs, desires
- Integration: “turning inward” for healing, reflection, insight

**External:**

- Actions in the world expressing internal integration
- Ethical, skillful, compassionate engagement with others

## 8️⃣ Order ↔ Chaos (Structure ↔ Spontaneity)

**Internal:**

- Mindset: predictability, habits, routines ↔ creativity, improvisation, intuition
- Integration: disciplined yet flexible mind, stability with adaptability

**External:**

- Life structure (planner, routines) vs responsive adaptation to reality
- Navigating unexpected events without losing coherence
