## If We Bring in the Five Aggregates (Buddhist)

From early Buddhist thought:

- Form
- Sensation
- Perception
- Mental formations
- Consciousness

Now look at this:

| Aggregate         | Rough Sense/Element Correspondence |

| ----------------- | ---------------------------------- |

| Form              | Earth (material structure)         |

| Sensation         | Water (contact, feeling-tone)      |

| Perception        | Fire (recognition, illumination)   |

| Mental Formations | Air (movement, thought currents)   |

| Consciousness     | Aether (field-like awareness)      |

Now is this historically linked to Thomas? No.

But structurally? The parallels are almost eerie.

They all describe:

1. Material base

2. Contact/feeling

3. Recognition

4. Movement/processing

5. Field of awareness

That’s a recurring pentadic architecture.
