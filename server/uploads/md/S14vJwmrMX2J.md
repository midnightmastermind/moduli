### External (Lab)

- Matter is broken down.
- Substance is heated until it blackens.
- Putrefaction.
- Decomposition.
- The original form is destroyed.

Alchemists believed you had to reduce the material to a kind of “prima materia” — the undifferentiated base substance.

### Internal (Psyche)

- Ego destabilization.
- Identity crisis.
- Depression / shadow confrontation.
- Loss of former worldview.
- “Dark night of the soul.”

This is when your constructed self falls apart.

You’ve actually been circling this phase a lot lately — that exhaustion, that loss of excitement, that feeling of being older than your age. That’s nigredo energy. It’s uncomfortable, but it’s not failure. It’s breakdown before reorganization.

**Outer formula:** break matter down.

**Inner formula:** break false identity down.
